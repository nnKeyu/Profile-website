About me

Hi! I’m Keyu (Wennie) Mao. I study how children perceive, trust, and cognitively engage with ariticial agents (e.g., robots, LLMs). Some of my research also involves adults.

I’m currently a research assistant at Zhejiang Normal University, where I earned my BA in Preschool Education. I work in a lab led by Dr Tingyu Li that brings together psychology and human–computer interaction. I also hold an MSc in Education (Child Development and Education) from the Department of Education, University of Oxford, where Professor Lars-Erik Malmberg supervised my dissertation.

I draw on developmental psychology and early childhood education to design behavioural studies of how people interact with artificial agents. I enjoy collaborating with people from different backgrounds and hearing how they approach the questions I study. Outside research, I enjoy cooking, listening to music, and travelling.
